About Run Pulse

Follow one illustrative pulse through three dams with fixed starting water levels. These presets show possible pathways, not a rule that every dam needs a bypass. In Science behind the screen, change the receiving dam’s storage to see when it can pass water without a bypass. Playback does not change the model assumptions.
